# Rental Photography & Video Studio — Automation & Growth Plan

*Prepared from strategy discussion, August 2026*

## Current State (How the Business Runs Today)

- **Website (Webflow):** Showcases props/backgrounds and how-to-use videos. No pricing shown by design.
- **Booking:** Calendly (with deposit collection) synced to Google Calendar. Sends a confirmation page with FAQs.
- **Pre-rental process:** Manual — you call renters a few days out to vet, confirm needs, and quote total price. No tracking system exists for who's been called.
- **On-site paperwork:** Rental agreement signed on iPad (almost no one pre-signs the emailed PDF). Photo ID and credit card photographed for security/legal protection.
- **Payment:** Square (iPad), or cash/Venmo/Apple Pay.
- **Post-rental:** Calendly automation sends a Google review reminder. You informally ask for photos/video, which you manually post to Instagram and the in-studio TV when received.
- **Leads:** Website "information request" form submits phone/email/description — currently untracked.
- **Marketing:** Instagram is primary; light Google presence; minimal Facebook.

## Key Pain Points Identified

1. No system tracks which bookings have been called/vetted — purely manual attention.
2. Paperwork (signature, ID, credit card) is handled in person, causing friction and pre-visit no-follow-through.
3. No centralized customer database — no visibility into repeat customers/lifetime value.
4. Content sourcing from customers is manual and inconsistent (no incentive to submit).
5. No system for culling, tagging, and posting the large volume of photo/video content sitting on your NAS.
6. Inbound leads from the website inquiry form aren't tracked or systematically followed up.
7. All customer communication (calls, texts) is manual — no automation despite many customers preferring text.

## Proposed Plan

### 1. Morning Operations Dashboard
A daily console (starting from Google Sheets, pulling structured data from Calendly) showing:
- Upcoming rentals needing a pre-visit call, with clear call-to-action status (called / not called)
- Flag for regular/repeat customers who may not need the full vetting call
- Inbound website inquiries (phone/email/description) with follow-up status, separate lane from bookings
- Goal: a 10-minute daily review replacing ad hoc attention-tracking

### 2. Digital Paperwork & ID Capture
- Move rental agreement signing to a pre-visit online e-signature flow (reduce on-site friction)
- Explore secure digital capture for ID/credit card verification ahead of arrival

### 3. Centralized Customer Database (CRM)
- Consolidate booking history, contact info, and regular/VIP status
- Potential to unify with your martial arts studio and tournament businesses later for a single customer view

### 4. Content Pipeline (Photo & Video Automation)
- Source: NAS library, organized into two categories — **"Shot At"** (finished/highlight content) and **"Behind the Scenes"**
- Automate: culling best images, tagging/hashtags/captions, scheduled posting to Instagram
- Track: what's been posted vs. not, with rotation logic to resurface older unposted/underused content and keep feed fresh
- Phase 2 (deferred): turning raw video into short-form content (Reels/Shorts)

### 5. Customer Content Submission Portal + Contest Incentive
- Replace informal "email me your photos" ask with a dedicated upload portal
- Add a monthly content contest/feature incentive to increase submission volume — submitted content becomes marketing fuel

### 6. SMS/Text Automation
- Explore automated text platforms for reminders, follow-ups, and generic communications
- Two-way texting so replies land in a trackable system rather than your personal phone
- Reflects customer preference for text over calls

### 7. Marketing Strategy
- Evaluate paid advertising fit: Instagram vs. Google vs. Facebook for this business type
- Define posting cadence and hashtag strategy alongside the content pipeline
- Consider seasonal/fall promotions to address slow periods
- Automated post-rental rebooking/follow-up sequences for past renters

### 8. Pricing Strategy
- Revisit the deliberate choice to omit pricing from the website — evaluate pros/cons given current lead flow

## Suggested Sequencing

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Morning dashboard (bookings + inquiries tracking) |
| 2 | Digital signing/ID capture |
| 3 | Content pipeline (culling, tagging, posting) + submission portal/contest |
| 4 | SMS automation for follow-ups |
| 5 | Paid marketing strategy + pricing review |
| 6 (later) | Video-to-shorts automation |
